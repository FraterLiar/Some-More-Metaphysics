Hello, I am a Liar.

When I was a kid this used to be my favorite paradox. If the statement is true, then I am not a Liar; but if I'm not a Liar, the statement is false.
The statement is empty of inherent meaning. It depends on ways of looking and predispositions. I take on this name because it is like a human being's identity: empty of inherent meaning also.

I’d like this website to be a grimoire of the mind. So-called ways of seeing that have changed my reality now and again and might benefit a reader. Little magic spells with serious philosophical firepower. But it is also meant to be a living document of my personal spiritual practice over the course of many years. I cannot commit to a regular schedule of writing, since this sort of thing evolves without any sort of notice, but it is meant to grow with my practice over time as new understandings crystallize.

My current practice encompasses Buddhist meditation, the various currents of the Western occult tradition (Frankensteined together using a chaos magic paradigm courtesy of St. Robert Anton Wilson), and the cultural context of the South Asian diaspora and Islam. Feel free to immerse yourself in darkness by putting out the candle and entering the ritual room, where I collect practical instructions. Or peruse one of the essays in the grimoire, where I synthesize personal experience with what I've read to shed interesting light on spiritual practice.
